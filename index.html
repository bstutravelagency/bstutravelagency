Places that stay with you

Discover a few of the destinations that make Sri Lanka so special. Choose a place that catches your eye and ask us about planning a visit.

Heritage & history

Sigiriya

Explore the iconic rock fortress, ancient gardens and stories of Sri Lanka's past.

Culture & tradition

Kandy

Experience a historic hill capital, scenic lake views and rich cultural traditions.

Hill country

Ella

Take in misty mountain views, lush tea country and beautiful walking trails.

Coast & relaxation

Mirissa

Unwind beside palm-fringed shores and enjoy the laid-back southern coast.

Wildlife & nature

Yala

Discover diverse habitats and the excitement of a wildlife safari experience.

Coastal heritage

Galle

Wander through the historic fort, charming streets and oceanfront viewpoints.
